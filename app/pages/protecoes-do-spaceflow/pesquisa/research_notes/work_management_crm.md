# Protections on built-in automations: work management, CRM and no-code database products (Pipefy, monday.com, ClickUp, Notion, Twenty, Airtable, HubSpot, Salesforce Flow)

Research date: 2026-10-24. Sources: official help centers where they were reachable. support.monday.com and help.clickup.com refuse WebFetch with 403, so those pages were read with curl and a browser user agent. Twenty was read from the source code on its `main` branch (sparse clone of github.com/twentyhq/twenty, 2026-10-24). A source marked "secondary" is a third-party blog or forum post.

## 1. Fixed vs configurable limits, with exact numbers

### Takeaway
All 8 products set their quotas and rate limits at plan or account level, not per automation. No end user can set a rate limit or quota on 1 automation, with 2 exceptions: Twenty (the self-host admin sets throttles through environment variables, per workspace) and HubSpot (webhook actions can carry their own rate limit). The per-automation "protections" a user sets are about behavior: re-enrollment, time windows, entry conditions and notifications.

### Cited Findings

**monday.com (automations + monday workflows)**
- Monthly action quota (official pricing): Pro 25,000 automation actions per month; Enterprise up to 250,000; Standard 250 (sources for Standard and Basic disagree) — [monday pricing](https://monday.com/pricing); [Activepieces, secondary](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/monday-com-pricing-2026-plans-costs-automation-limits)
- Workflows and automations share 1 action limit ("one for Automations, the workflow builder, and apps"); integrations have a separate limit. "executing condition steps also counts toward and consumes from your action quota." A notification uses 1 action per person notified, an email 1 action per email sent — [monday: active workflows and actions](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/11381357489810-monday-workflows-active-workflows-and-actions)
- Overage on Pro: "the number of additional actions used over the limit will be deducted from the next month's allocation" (example: 28,000 used in April leaves 22,000 for May). When the whole month is used up, "the account will be blocked from editing or adding new Workflow blocks, Automations, and monday apps." The admin gets an email warning at "X% of the total available actions" — [same article](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/11381357489810-monday-workflows-active-workflows-and-actions)
- Active workflow cap (monday AI work platform): Standard 3, Pro 20, Enterprise 250. CRM, service and dev: Pro 5, Enterprise 250. Add-on for more. The toggle is the "Status switcher" in the Workflow center / Autopilot hub, Workflows tab — [same article](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/11381357489810-monday-workflows-active-workflows-and-actions)
- Fixed per-minute rate limits (official), with 2 tiers. "Throttled: automations continue running but may be slower." "Blocked: no new triggers/actions are accepted until the limit resets. Incoming runs will fail with errors, which may raise the automation's error rate and lead to auto-disable." — [monday: Automation and integration rate limits](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/9060097050258-Automation-and-integration-rate-limits)
  - Triggers per automation: 250 / 60s throttle, 4,500 / 60s block
  - Triggers per board: 500 / 60s throttle, 10,000 / 60s block
  - Triggers per account: 2,000 / 60s throttle, 100,000 / 60s block
  - Actions per automation: 500 / 60s throttle, 10,000 / 60s block
  - Actions per board: 1,000 / 60s throttle, 30,000 / 60s block
  - Actions per account: 2,500 / 60s throttle, 250,000 / 60s block
- "Complexity limits": per account, reset every 60 seconds, "regardless of the plan type." They weigh heavy actions such as creating subitems, batch actions, mirroring and duplicating boards. Message text: "One of your automations has failed. Notice: Too many automatons were running at the same time. Click here to view it." (typo as published). The action "may be delayed or fully canceled" — [same article](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/9060097050258-Automation-and-integration-rate-limits)

**ClickUp Automations**

"Action limits reset on the first of every month (PST)." — [ClickUp: Automations feature availability and limits](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/23477062949911-Automations-feature-availability-and-limits)
- At the cap: "When your limit has been exceeded, your Workspace's Automations are paused for that month." Owners and admins get usage alert emails at 90% and 100% — [same](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/23477062949911-Automations-feature-availability-and-limits)
- Structural limit per automation: Triggers 1, Conditions 15 (Business and above), Actions 6 — [same](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/23477062949911-Automations-feature-availability-and-limits)
- Usage history retention: Free Forever, Unlimited and Business 1 day; Business Plus 30 days; Enterprise 6 months — [same](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/23477062949911-Automations-feature-availability-and-limits)
- Third-party sources quote other numbers for Business (10,000 and 1,000). They conflict with the official 5,000 — [ClickUp official](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/23477062949911-Automations-feature-availability-and-limits) vs secondary results in search

**Airtable automations**
- Official limits per plan: Free 100 runs per month per workspace, 75 automations per base, 2 weeks of run history; Team 25,000 / 100 / 6 months; Business 100,000 / 150 / 1 year; Enterprise Scale 500,000 / 200 / 3 years. A turned-off automation still takes a slot; "Deleting frees a slot." "both failed and successful automation attempts will count." Limits "reset on the first day of each month" — [Airtable: Getting started with automations](https://support.airtable.com/articles/3669392397-getting-started-with-airtable-automations)
- Failed and canceled runs count against the allowance — [Airtable plans overview](https://support.airtable.com/articles/2277136852-airtable-plans-overview)
- Other fixed limits: scripts hit a "30 seconds network timeout error"; "Update record" updates 1 record per run — [Airtable: Troubleshooting automations](https://support.airtable.com/articles/6756755850-troubleshooting-airtable-automations)
- Usage alert emails to the owner at 80%, 90% and 100% (secondary, not confirmed in official docs) — [Noloco, secondary](https://noloco.io/blog/airtable-automation-limit)

**Pipefy**
- "Automation jobs" per month (official): Starter (Free) 15; Business 300; Enterprise 2,000; Unlimited 15 MM. "Automation rules" (the automations themselves) are unlimited — [Pipefy: How Pipefy enumerates automation jobs vs automation rules](https://help.pipefy.com/en/articles/6445865-how-pipefy-enumerates-automation-jobs-vs-automation-rules)
- A job counts every time the trigger event fires, even when the condition fails: "The verification itself is an automation job, even if the action doesn't happen." A recurring automation every 30 minutes is billed 1 job every 30 minutes. Admins see usage in real time on the "Usage stats" page — [same](https://help.pipefy.com/en/articles/6445865-how-pipefy-enumerates-automation-jobs-vs-automation-rules)
- Over the limit: buy add-ons or upgrade. Add-ons: 500 for $26 (Business); 1,000 for $48, 10,000 for $319, 50,000 for $1,068 (Enterprise / Unlimited). API calls: 20 / 500 / 10,000 / 15 million per plan — [Pipefy: What to do when reaching your automation, integration, or API limit](https://help.pipefy.com/en/articles/5419654-what-to-do-when-reaching-your-automation-integration-or-api-limit)

**Notion (database automations and buttons)**
- Database automations need a paid plan. Free users can only create Slack notification automations and can use, but not edit, template automations. Only paid users with full database access can create, edit or delete automations — [Notion: Database automations](https://www.notion.com/help/database-automations)
- The page states no maximum automations per database and no rate limit. Gmail's own sending limits apply; emails can take up to 2 minutes — [same](https://www.notion.com/help/database-automations)

**Twenty CRM (open source, workflows)**
- Throttles are environment variables, group `RATE_LIMITING`, set by the instance admin, not in the workflow UI — [twenty config-variables.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/engine/core-modules/twenty-config/config-variables.ts)
  - `WORKFLOW_EXEC_SOFT_THROTTLE_LIMIT = 100` with `WORKFLOW_EXEC_SOFT_THROTTLE_TTL = 60_000` ms: "Throttle limit for workflow execution. Remaining will not be enqueued immediately."
  - `WORKFLOW_EXEC_HARD_THROTTLE_LIMIT = 5000` with `WORKFLOW_EXEC_HARD_THROTTLE_TTL = 3_600_000` ms: "Remaining will be marked as failed."
- Both are token buckets keyed per workspace (`workflow:execution-soft-throttle:<workspaceId>`, `workflow:execution-hard-throttle:<workspaceId>`), not per workflow — [workflow-throttling.workspace-service.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/modules/workflow/workflow-runner/workflow-run-queue/workspace-services/workflow-throttling.workspace-service.ts)
- History: PR #9263 (Dec 2024) first added a per-workflow throttle of "10 executions / sec by default" and the exception code `WORKFLOW_RUN_LIMIT_REACHED`. The current code replaced it with the per-workspace soft and hard buckets above — [PR #9263](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/pull/9263); current code as cited
- Iterator (loop) node: `MAX_ITERATIONS = 10000`, error "Iterator has reached the maximum number of iterations" — [iterator.workflow-action.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/modules/workflow/workflow-executor/workflow-actions/iterator/iterator.workflow-action.ts)
- Housekeeping constants: `NUMBER_OF_WORKFLOW_RUNS_TO_KEEP = 1000`; `RUNS_TO_CLEAN_THRESHOLD_DAYS = 14`; stale and stuck run thresholds of 1 hour (`STALED_RUNS_THRESHOLD_MS`, `STUCK_RUNNING_RUNS_THRESHOLD_MS`, `STUCK_STOPPING_RUNS_THRESHOLD_MS` = 60 × 60 × 1000) — [workflow-run-queue/constants](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/tree/main/packages/twenty-server/src/modules/workflow/workflow-runner/workflow-run-queue/constants)
- Cloud billing uses "workflow credits": the free trial without a credit card gets 500,000 microCredits, with a card 2,500,000 — [config-variables.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/engine/core-modules/twenty-config/config-variables.ts)
- A fork (mmp-8001/twenty, issue #7) quotes the same 100 runs/min soft and 5,000 runs/hour hard values, which matches upstream `main` — [fork issue, secondary](https://github.com/mmp-8001/twenty/issues/7)

**HubSpot workflows**
- The "Settings" panel holds no numeric quota. Timing and notification options are listed in section 3 — [HubSpot: Manage workflow settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manage-your-workflow-settings)
- A rate limit the user sets on an action can pause it: "If your action is paused due to the rate limit, it will not execute," and the error shows in the action logs (search snippet of HubSpot docs) — [HubSpot: webhooks in workflows](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/how-do-i-use-webhooks-with-hubspot-workflows)
- AI actions that need credits: "This action's feature is paused. Either you're out of credits or your admin paused usage." The workflow retries "once every 24 hours, up to 60 days" — [HubSpot: Troubleshoot common workflow errors](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/troubleshoot-common-workflow-errors)

**Salesforce Flow**
- Fixed governor limits exist "to prevent runaway automations from monopolizing shared resources." Async flow paths share daily async limits and the "org-wide 24-hour limit on asynchronous executions." Up to 50 queueable jobs can be enqueued from a synchronous trigger — [Salesforce Architects: Record-Triggered Automation decision guide](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/decision-guides/guide/record-triggered)
- A limits guide quotes a cap of 12 duplicate updates to the same record per batch (secondary, not verified on help.salesforce.com) — [SalesforceBen, secondary](https://www.salesforceben.com/complete-guide-to-salesforce-flow-limits-and-how-to-avoid-them/)

### Inferences
- Common pattern: the quota (runs or actions per month) and the rate limit (per minute or per hour) belong to the account or workspace. The automation only holds behavioral settings. A "Protections" tab per flow in Spaceflow would be closer to HubSpot's Settings panel than to any quota screen, unless ENSPACE lets the admin set a per-flow cap. None of the 8 products offers that cap to business users, except HubSpot's per-action rate limit.
- monday is the only product that publishes per-automation rate limits, with 2 explicit tiers: slow down first (throttle), then reject (block, then auto-disable). It is a ready model for a Spaceflow "excess" policy.
- Pipefy and Airtable both count runs that do nothing (condition false, failed, canceled). That makes loop protection a cost issue as well as a stability issue.

### Gaps
- monday Basic and Standard quotas: sources conflict; the official pricing page renders with JS and was not read in full.
- Salesforce's official numeric limits (schedule-triggered flow interviews per 24 h, flow element limits) were not readable: help.salesforce.com pages render with JS and WebFetch returned only navigation.
- HubSpot: no per-plan quota of workflow actions was found; the configurable action rate limit was seen only in a search snippet, without its exact UI label.

## 2. Loop and recursion protection (A triggers B triggers A)

### Takeaway
There are 4 strategies. (1) Hard block: Notion's database automations "can't be triggered by other automations". (2) Detect and warn at build time: monday warns but lets the user save, and Pipefy marks event and action pairs that could loop. (3) Re-enrollment off by default plus entry conditions: HubSpot, and Salesforce's "only when updated to meet conditions" with `$Record__Prior`. (4) Nothing structural, only rate limits and quotas: Airtable, ClickUp and Twenty.

### Cited Findings
- **Notion:** "Database automations can't be triggered by other automations." A button action can trigger them: a user who clicks a button that creates a page fires the automation, but a recurring template does not — [Notion: Database automations](https://www.notion.com/help/database-automations)
- **monday:** "Automation loop detection. If you are about to create an automation that will cause an endless loop, you will see the following warning... You can create the automation anyway, but an endless loop can use many automation actions and may slow down your account. Proceed with caution." "Automation Loop" is a listed reason for a failed or deactivated automation — [monday: Why is my automation deactivated?](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010415679-Why-is-my-automation-deactivated)
- **monday:** a runtime loop hits the per-automation rate limit. "If you have an automation template that reached this limit, that template will be disabled and you'll see an error message appear in the board's Automation page." The user turns it back on in the board's Automation Center — [same](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010415679-Why-is-my-automation-deactivated); [monday rate limits](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/9060097050258-Automation-and-integration-rate-limits)
- **monday:** an email sent from a board creates an update, which can re-fire an "update is created" automation — [monday: Gmail integration troubleshooting](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013327160-Gmail-integration-troubleshooting). A blogger reports runtime loops slowed to about 1 update every 1 to 2 s per item and says monday has no real cycle detection (secondary) — [dev.to, secondary](https://dev.to/piotrdiuk/infinite-cycles-in-mondaycom-automations-51kp). A claim that monday blocks one automation from firing another (secondary) conflicts with monday's own loop warning and loop-failure docs — [TaskRhino, secondary](https://www.taskrhino.ca/blog/monday-com-one-trigger-multiple-actions/) vs [monday official](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010415679-Why-is-my-automation-deactivated)
- **Pipefy:** the automation actions API returns `eventsBlacklist` ("A list of events that cannot trigger the action") and `triggerEvents` ("A list of events that can relate to the action in a way that could generate an infinite loop of automations"). Examples: `send_a_task` blacklists `scheduler`, `sla_based`, `card_left_phase` and `manually_triggered`; `generate_with_ai` blacklists `scheduler` and lists `field_updated` as a loop-risk event — [Pipefy developers: Automation Actions](https://developers.pipefy.com/reference/automation-actions)
- **Airtable:** no loop guard. Loops happen when an action re-fires its own trigger ("Create a new record" feeding "When a record enters a view"), or when 2 or more automations in a base fire each other. A looping automation runs until you have "exhausted your workspace plan limits" — [Airtable: Troubleshooting automations](https://support.airtable.com/articles/6756755850-troubleshooting-airtable-automations)
- **ClickUp:** loop warning in the docs only. "If the applied template creates new subtasks and you're using the create task trigger, an automation loop may start. To end the loop, disable the trigger." — [ClickUp: Use Automation Actions](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/6312097314199-Use-Automation-Actions). An action of one automation can fire another (ClickUp blog) — [ClickUp blog](https://clickup.com/blog/automation-examples/)
- **Twenty:** no self-trigger guard. The database-event listener ignores an event only when `workspaceId` or the event name is missing (`shouldIgnoreEvent`). It fires when the update touches a watched field (`settings.fields`; an empty list means any field) and the record filter matches. Runs started by a workflow are stamped `createdBy.source = WORKFLOW` (`FieldActorSource.WORKFLOW`), but the trigger does not filter on it. Runaway chains stop only at the soft and hard throttles — [workflow-database-event-trigger.listener.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/modules/workflow/workflow-trigger/automated-trigger/listeners/workflow-database-event-trigger.listener.ts); [build-workflow-run-source.util.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/modules/workflow/workflow-trigger/utils/build-workflow-run-source.util.ts)
- **HubSpot:** re-enrollment is off by default. A record that already went through a workflow re-enters only when it meets explicit re-enrollment triggers. To block re-entry for good, use a suppression list or unenrollment triggers — [HubSpot: Manually unenroll records](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manually-unenroll-objects-from-workflows) (via search snippet)
- **HubSpot:** "Unenroll contacts from other workflows when enrolled in this workflow" (Connections section; contact workflows only) is off by default, with options "Unenroll from all other workflows" or "Unenroll from specific workflows" — [HubSpot: Manage workflow settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manage-your-workflow-settings)
- **Salesforce:** the official guidance tells builders to stop recursion by "setting the flow to run only when the record is updated to meet the condition requirements" and by comparing with `$Record__Prior`. Before-save updates avoid the second save that would "re-execute the entire save order and fire all automation again." "state cannot be cached or shared between different flow triggers." No depth number is stated — [Salesforce Architects decision guide](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/decision-guides/guide/record-triggered)
- **Salesforce:** the entry option "Only when a record is updated to meet the condition requirements" is the usual anti-recursion setting. In the order of execution, a recursive save skips a block of steps (secondary) — [Agentforcelens, secondary](https://agentforcelens.com/en/article/salesforce-order-of-execution-guide); [sfdcdevelopers, secondary](https://sfdcdevelopers.com/2025/11/17/how-to-trigger-a-record-triggered-flow/)

### Inferences
- Build-time warning (monday, Pipefy) plus runtime ceiling (rate limit, then auto-disable) is the most common pair for business-user tools. Notion's hard block is the simplest, but it rules out legitimate chains.
- HubSpot's "re-enroll only on explicit triggers" and Salesforce's "only when updated to meet conditions" are the same idea as "run once per record until the condition becomes false and then true again". They fit a per-flow toggle in a Protections tab.
- Twenty marks the run source (`WORKFLOW`) but does not use it to block triggers. ENSPACE could use the same marker to offer an option such as "Ignore changes made by other flows".

### Gaps
- Pipefy's help center has no article on runtime loop behavior. The UI wording for the loop warning (if any) was not found; only the API fields are documented.
- No product publishes a maximum chain depth (A to B to C). Salesforce has a transaction limit, but the exact recursion count was not found in official docs.
- ClickUp: no official statement on whether loops are detected or only bounded by the monthly quota.

## 3. Per-automation settings (re-enrollment, run once per record, schedule windows, unenrollment, suppression, pause, turn off after errors)

### Takeaway
HubSpot has the richest per-workflow settings panel ("Settings": timing windows, blackout dates, auto turn-off date, unenroll from other workflows, performance alerts, plus enrollment-side re-enrollment, suppression and unenrollment). The others offer an On/Off toggle and owner or notification options, and the "run only when the condition becomes true" behavior lives in the trigger.

### Cited Findings
- **HubSpot — workflow "Settings"** (open the workflow, click **Settings** at top left, right panel) — [HubSpot: Manage workflow settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manage-your-workflow-settings):
  - "Run actions at specific times only": chosen days and hours in the account time zone. Actions outside the window are rescheduled to the next available time, within a 15-minute window.
  - "Don't run actions on specific dates": pauses actions on chosen dates (can repeat yearly); records still enroll.
  - "Schedule workflow to turn off automatically": turns the workflow off at a set date and time.
  - Time zone: the account time zone; EST when none is set. Timing affects actions only, not enrollment.
  - Performance (Enterprise): "Get notified when this workflow needs review"; "Get notified if the enrollment rate changes" (Breeze Intelligence or a manual range: check count weekly or daily, "Increased at least", "Decreased by at least"); recipients "Send to these users" / "Send to these teams"; "Compare conversion for each branch and end-point".
  - Connections (contact workflows): "Unenroll contacts from other workflows when enrolled in this workflow".
- **HubSpot — enrollment side:** suppression lists and unenrollment triggers "will appear in a Settings tab" when you configure enrollment triggers. A suppression list blocks enrollment even when the trigger is met; active and static lists both work. Suppression blocks future enrollment and does not remove records already enrolled; unenrollment triggers remove them. Contacts removed from a suppression segment do not auto-enroll — [HubSpot: Set unenrollment triggers](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/set-unenrollment-triggers-in-company-deal-ticket-quote-based-workflows); [DNA Tech, secondary](https://dnatechsolutions.com/blog/effective-workflow-management-hubspot-enrollment-suppression-and-unenrollment)
- **HubSpot — time window message:** "Outside time window settings. Action rescheduled for [new date and time]" — [HubSpot: Troubleshoot common workflow errors](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/troubleshoot-common-workflow-errors)
- **Salesforce Flow:** "run only when updated to meet conditions" is an entry option of the record-triggered flow (see section 2). The flow's error email recipient is set org-wide, not per flow (see section 4) — [Salesforce Architects](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/decision-guides/guide/record-triggered)
- **monday:** per-automation "Transfer ownership" (three-dot menu on the board's Automations page); account-level default owner, "Keep automations running when users are deactivated"; Administration > Directory > "Automations ownership" — [monday: Why is my automation deactivated?](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010415679-Why-is-my-automation-deactivated). Workflows have an active/inactive "Status switcher" in the Workflow center — [monday workflows](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/11381357489810-monday-workflows-active-workflows-and-actions)
- **Pipefy:** each automation has a toggle (blue = active, gray = inactive); duplicated automations start disabled; filter by Status (Active / Inactive), Event and Action; list shows last updated date and last modifier; org admins see automations across all pipes — [Pipefy: How to manage automations](https://help.pipefy.com/en/articles/4991746-how-to-manage-automations-in-pipefy)
- **Airtable:** On/Off toggle per automation; "Notifications" settings per automation where subscribers can be added (Creator permission or higher on the base) — [Airtable: Troubleshooting automations](https://support.airtable.com/articles/6756755850-troubleshooting-airtable-automations)
- **Notion:** per automation, hover then "•••" to edit, pause or delete; "Active" toggle. Triggers come from the ⚡ icon or the slider icon > "Automations". Behavioral limits: multiple "is edited" triggers must happen within about 3 seconds; the "Every" trigger can't be combined with other triggers; pages with restricted access are skipped; a locked database won't fire automations — [Notion: Database automations](https://www.notion.com/help/database-automations)
- **Twenty:** update triggers can watch specific fields (`fields`) and carry a record filter (`stepFilters`, `stepFilterGroups`); cron triggers take a `pattern`. No re-enrollment, time window or error-threshold setting exists in the trigger settings types — [automated-trigger-settings.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/modules/workflow/workflow-trigger/automated-trigger/constants/automated-trigger-settings.ts)
- **ClickUp:** automations run as the user who created them (secondary) — [Bell Consulting, secondary](https://www.bellconsultingsolutions.com/post/clickup-automations-not-working)

### Inferences
- Candidate Spaceflow per-flow options, by the products that ship them: time window and blackout dates (HubSpot); auto turn-off date (HubSpot); re-entry rule per item (HubSpot re-enrollment, Salesforce "only when updated to meet conditions"); fields to watch (Twenty, Salesforce `$Record__Prior`); fallback owner (monday); failure subscribers (Airtable); enrollment-rate anomaly alert (HubSpot Enterprise).
- None of the products offers a user-set "turn off after N failures". Auto-disable is always a platform rule (monday rate block, Notion pause on error).

### Gaps
- Exact current UI labels for HubSpot re-enrollment ("Re-enroll" toggle per trigger) were not confirmed on the official page; the re-enrollment article was not fetched.
- ClickUp and Airtable: no official per-automation schedule window or re-entry option found.

## 4. Error handling: run history, failure notifications, auto-pause, retry

### Takeaway
Failure email goes to 1 person tied to the automation (Airtable: who last turned it on; Salesforce: who last modified the flow; monday: notification to the owner). Auto-disable exists in Notion (any runtime error pauses the automation), monday (rate-limit block, loop, item limit, deactivated owner or account) and Airtable (authentication problems). Retries are explicit only in HubSpot (webhooks for 3 days; AI actions every 24 h for 60 days) and monday (item limit: retry the next minute; throttled runs delayed).

### Cited Findings
- **Airtable:** the email subject is "Something went wrong with an automation"; it goes to the person who turned the automation On. If that person leaves, all workspace owners receive it. Extra subscribers go in the automation's Notifications settings. Run statuses: "Ran successfully", "Pending", "Run cancelled", "Failed run". Automations can "independently toggle 'Off'" after an authentication problem, such as a password change in a connected account. Repeated failures alone are not documented to turn an automation off — [Airtable: Troubleshooting automations](https://support.airtable.com/articles/6756755850-troubleshooting-airtable-automations)
- **Airtable:** a third-party tutorial claims auto turn-off after repeated consecutive failures (unverified) — [BaseBrainers, secondary](https://basebrainers.com/tutorials/airtable-automation-monitoring-error-handling). A rerun executes the configuration "as it stood when that run was first attempted" — [Airtable: Send email action](https://support.airtable.com/articles/1091012569-airtable-automation-action-send-email)
- **Notion:** errors at build time block saving. When a working automation starts failing, "the automation is paused." Users are notified for some errors (missing database or property, invalid or expired Gmail or Slack connection) but "may not be notified" for formula or webhook errors. The automation stays paused until a user turns "Active" back on — [Notion: Database automations](https://www.notion.com/help/database-automations)
- **monday:** deactivation causes: deactivated user, unauthorized user, assignee lost access, deleted group/column/board/workspace, "Automation Loop", "Item Limit Reached", "Rate limit reached", deactivated account (2 or more weeks: "Your automation was disabled due to your account being deactivated."). Some causes send no notification: "The automation owner was deactivated from your account." shows only on the board's Automation page. Item limit: "it will retry in the next minute." Rate limit: "you'll receive a notification to notify you that your automation or integration has failed to run... it may run eventually with a delay," and the delay shows in the automation "Run history." Detail lives in "Automations Activity" in the Automation Center — [monday: Why is my automation deactivated?](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010415679-Why-is-my-automation-deactivated); [monday rate limits](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/9060097050258-Automation-and-integration-rate-limits)
- **HubSpot:** failed webhooks retry for 3 days, starting 1 minute after the failure, at increasing intervals up to 8 hours apart. No retry on 4XX except 429, which honors `Retry-After` — [HubSpot: webhooks in workflows](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/how-do-i-use-webhooks-with-hubspot-workflows). Custom code actions retry up to 3 days for 429 and 5XX errors, but only when the code throws the error — [HubSpot developers: custom code actions](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api/workflows/custom-code-actions). Log messages: "Action failed because HubSpot is being rate limited by the connected app, but will retry soon."; "Webhook wasn't able to execute, but will retry soon." — [HubSpot: Troubleshoot common workflow errors](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/troubleshoot-common-workflow-errors)
- **HubSpot:** proactive alerts ("Get notified when this workflow needs review", enrollment-rate change) go to the users or teams chosen in Settings (Enterprise) — [HubSpot: Manage workflow settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manage-your-workflow-settings)
- **Salesforce:** Setup > Process Automation Settings, field "Send Process or Flow Error Email to" (wording varies by release). Options: "User Who Last Modified the Process or Flow" (default) or "Apex Exception Email Recipients" (addresses on the Apex Exception Email page) — [Salesforce Help: Select Flow and Process Error Email Recipients](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?language=en_US&id=platform.flow_troubleshoot_error_email.htm&type=5)
- **Salesforce:** a synchronous limit failure "will cause the user's entire save transaction to roll back." Flow has "built-in protections, such as auto-bulkification and automatic retries." The "Custom Error" element can block a save and show a message, but not in every flow type — [Salesforce Architects](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/decision-guides/guide/record-triggered)
- **ClickUp:** usage tracking in the Usage tab of Manage Automations, which "updates every ten automation uses" (secondary). History retention by plan is in section 1 — [Bell Consulting, secondary](https://www.bellconsultingsolutions.com/post/clickup-automations-not-working); [ClickUp official](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/23477062949911-Automations-feature-availability-and-limits)
- **Twenty:** a hard-throttled run is created with status `FAILED` and error "Throttle limit reached" (`createCoreWorkflowRun`), and the metric `WorkflowRunThrottled` is incremented. Retry exists per step and for iterators (`build-retry-step-infos.util.ts`, `build-retry-iterator-step-infos.util.ts`). A cron job handles runs stale or stuck for more than 1 hour — [core-workflow-runner.service.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/modules/workflow/workflow-runner/services/core-workflow-runner.service.ts); [workflow-runner utils](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/tree/main/packages/twenty-server/src/modules/workflow/workflow-runner/utils)

### Inferences
- The failure recipient is almost always an implicit person (last enabler, last modifier, owner). Only Airtable (subscribers) and HubSpot (users or teams) let you pick recipients. A "Notify on failure: users/teams" field in Spaceflow matches that pattern.
- Silent deactivation is a known pain: monday says some causes send no notification. A Protections tab could show the current state and the reason for an automatic pause.

### Gaps
- Pipefy: no official doc on per-automation error logs, failure notifications or auto-disable was found in this pass. Its management article does not cover them.
- ClickUp: no official doc on failure notifications or auto-disable of a single automation, other than the quota-wide pause.
- Twenty's UI labels for run status and retry were not checked (frontend not cloned).

## 5. What happens to excess runs

### Takeaway
Behaviors vary: queue and delay (Twenty soft throttle, monday throttle, HubSpot rescheduling), fail with a recorded error (Twenty hard throttle, monday block), pause everything (ClickUp at the monthly cap), block editing but borrow from next month (monday Pro), and stop until the reset (Airtable, Pipefy). Failed and no-op runs count against quota in Airtable and Pipefy.

### Cited Findings
- **Twenty:** non-manual runs are created as `NOT_STARTED` and handed to an enqueue job. The soft bucket (100 per 60 s per workspace) limits how many enter the queue; the rest wait ("Remaining will not be enqueued immediately"). Beyond the hard bucket (5,000 per hour per workspace) a run is saved as `FAILED` with "Throttle limit reached". Manual-trigger runs skip the soft queue (`ENQUEUED` directly) but still pass the hard check — [core-workflow-runner.service.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/modules/workflow/workflow-runner/services/core-workflow-runner.service.ts); [config-variables.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-server/src/engine/core-modules/twenty-config/config-variables.ts)
- **monday:** throttle means slower runs; block means "Incoming runs will fail with errors, which may raise the automation's error rate and lead to auto-disable." The monthly quota on Pro borrows from next month; when the month is used up, editing and adding automations is blocked — [monday rate limits](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/9060097050258-Automation-and-integration-rate-limits); [monday workflows](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/11381357489810-monday-workflows-active-workflows-and-actions)
- **ClickUp:** "When your limit has been exceeded, your Workspace's Automations are paused for that month." — [ClickUp](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/23477062949911-Automations-feature-availability-and-limits)
- **Airtable:** runs are gated by the monthly limit and resume after the reset on the first of the month. When the limit is reached, the automation can't create records until the reset. Failed runs count — [Airtable troubleshooting](https://support.airtable.com/articles/6756755850-troubleshooting-airtable-automations); [Airtable getting started](https://support.airtable.com/articles/3669392397-getting-started-with-airtable-automations)
- **Pipefy:** every trigger check is billed; at the cap, buy add-ons or upgrade. What happens to the jobs themselves (skipped or queued) is not stated — [Pipefy jobs](https://help.pipefy.com/en/articles/6445865-how-pipefy-enumerates-automation-jobs-vs-automation-rules); [Pipefy limits](https://help.pipefy.com/en/articles/5419654-what-to-do-when-reaching-your-automation-integration-or-api-limit)
- **HubSpot:** actions outside the time window are rescheduled to the next allowed time; rate-limited actions pause and resume later — [HubSpot settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manage-your-workflow-settings); [HubSpot webhooks](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/how-do-i-use-webhooks-with-hubspot-workflows)
- **Salesforce:** exceeding a synchronous governor limit throws a `LimitException` and rolls back the whole save — [Salesforce Architects](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/decision-guides/guide/record-triggered)

### Inferences
- Twenty's 2 tiers (queue first, fail later) and monday's 2 tiers (throttle, then block and auto-disable) are the same design. A Spaceflow "excess runs" policy could offer "Queue" vs "Discard and record failure" with a platform ceiling above it.
- Salesforce's rollback is the outlier: the user's own save fails. Business-user tools avoid that by running automations asynchronously.

### Gaps
- Pipefy and Notion: behavior of the excess runs themselves (queued, dropped, failed) is undocumented.
- Whether monday's "error rate" auto-disable has a published threshold: none found.
